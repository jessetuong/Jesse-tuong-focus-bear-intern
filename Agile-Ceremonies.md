# Research & Learn
- Main Agile Ceremonies: 
1. Daily stand-ups: short (10–15 min) sync where each person shares what they did, what's next, and any challenges
2. Sprint Planning (Scrum): team commits to a fixed set of work for a defined sprint which is a fixed range of time (2-3 weeks)
3. Continuous Prioritization (Kanban): no fixed sprints — work is pulled continuously from a prioritized backlog as capacity frees up, better suited to fast-changing priorities
4. Retrospectives: team reflects on what went well/poorly after a sprint or period, and agrees on improvements
5. Backlog Refinement: ongoing process of clarifying, estimating, and prioritizing upcoming work so it's ready to be picked up
- Async collaboration and cross-timezone collaboration: Written async stand-up updates (Slack/Discord thread) instead of live meetings, Recorded video updates for anyone who can't attend live, Shared docs/boards (e.g., Trello, Linear, Notion) as the single source of truth, updated continuously rather than relying on meetings, Clear written context in tickets/PRs so people in other time zones aren't blocked waiting for a live conversation

# Reflection 
- Ceremonies help alignment by surfacing blockers early, keeping everyone's work visible, and creating regular checkpoints for course-correction rather than discovering problems at the end
- Most important for an intern/dev role: likely the stand-up — it's the lowest-effort, highest-frequency way to stay visible, unblock quickly, and understand what the rest of the team is doing
