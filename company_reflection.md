# Reflection on the company mission
- After researching about the company mission, I acknowledged that Focus Bear was created in mind to help ND folks to focus on their studies and work. Specifically, after trying other productivity and wellness apps and was not happy with their performance, the Focus Bear team - who are ND, and usually experience the productivity and wellbeing challenges - decided to build Focus Bear.
- Focus Bear aims to solve the concentration problem - which is very common in this era due to increased awareness - that is badly affecting how people perform in their work and study. 
- This mission is extremely crucial since ADHD diagnosis is skyrocketing at the moment, and the effect ADHD has on people's work and study is severe - from stopping people from focusing into a specific work to the increasing needs of social media to increase dopamine. Also, there are many people who are struggled to focus on their work and study without being diagnosis of ADHD since there are many distractions a person can get in this technological era (e.g social media, video games,..). This makes the number of individuals that need to focus better in their work and study way much more than we expected, and Focus Bear is trying to solve this.
- Personally, I'm usually struggled with focusing on my study and work myself. Specifically, I'm ussually distracted by small things such as phone notifications, or social media apps like Tiktok - that's how I relate to these challenges personally. Thus, developing a wellbeing app with Focus Bear is also helping me to build a solution for myself. Also, it can also help other people with same issues overcome their challenges. That's how Focus Bear's mission (to help ND minds and easily-distracted minds to focus) aligns with my interests in helping other people that are struggling to focus like me to concentrate on their work and studies, and to make the most out of their time.
