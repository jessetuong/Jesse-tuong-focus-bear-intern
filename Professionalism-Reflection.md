# Research & Learn:
- Professional examples could be: write email in professional format, wording, texting, or talking to other colleagues in respectful language. Unprofessional would be ghosting messages, venting/complaining publicly, interrupting or talking over others.
- Respectful communciation in remote teams: Neutral, clear tone in writing, disagree with the idea, not the person; give reasons, not just objections, respond to feedback with curiosity, not defensiveness.
- Giving/Receiving feedbacks professionally: focus on specific actions/outcomes, not personality, assume good intent before reacting, ask clarifying questions instead of getting defensive.

# Reflection:
- Great teamwork examples: usually driven by clear roles, fast/honest communication, and people covering for each other without being asked.
- Steps to ensure lear/respectful/inclusive communication: write concisely, avoid jargon, confirm understanding, be mindful of time zones and async delays.
- How can I help create positive environment: celebrate small wins, smile with colleagues when possible, avoid expressing negative attitude in public with colleagues, be approachable, don't dump negativity on the team.
- Meeting prep: review agenda beforehand, bring specific questions/updates, know what decision or outcome you need.
- Being proactive: flag blockers early, suggest solutions not just problems, ask for more work when idle, document what you learn.
- Following up: polite nudge after a reasonable wait (e.g., 1–2 days), then escalate to a supervisor if it's blocking real progress or after 2+ unanswered follow-ups.
- Things you won't do: won't gossip, won't ignore messages for days.
