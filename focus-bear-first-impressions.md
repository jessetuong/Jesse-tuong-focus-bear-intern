# Confusing or unclear points:
- After trying the app for a week, I concluded 3 points that I think it is confusing and could be improved.
- The first one is the "Day plan" list and the "To do list". I'm not sure what are the differences between them and also there were no clear description of their differences.
- The second one is the "Log in with Google Account" option on Mac version. Specifically, when I clicked on that one, I got navigated to the browser to log in. After filling in all the credentials, I was navigated back to the Focus Bear client and stayed logged out - there were no error codes showed. I think this is worth t be checked again by the Focus Bear team.
- The third one is the design "Back to today" button below the date label on top of the page in the "Day plan" that could be improved. Specifically, it is formatted as a line of blue words with an underline - which seems not professional. Personally, I think that one could be re-formatted to a button, which will make it seem way more consistent with the whole page's design. i will attach the photo of the page in the comment section on Github.
