# Confusing or unclear points:
- After trying the app for a week, I concluded 5 points that I think it is confusing and could be improved.
- The first one is the "Day plan" list and the "To do list". I'm not sure what are the differences between them and also there were no clear description of their differences. I expect to acknowledge the difference between them in the setup guidance process when I first downloaded the app.
- The second one is the "Log in with Google Account" option on Mac version. Specifically, when I clicked on that one, I got navigated to the browser to log in. After filling in all the credentials, I was navigated back to the Focus Bear client and stayed logged out - there were no error codes showed. I think this is a potential bug that is worth to be checked again by the Focus Bear team. 
- The third one is the design "Back to today" button below the date label on top of the page in the "Day plan" that could be improved. Specifically, it is formatted as a line of blue words with an underline - which seems not professional. Personally, I think that one could be re-formatted to a button, which will make it seem way more consistent with the whole page's design. I will attach the photo of the page in the comment section on Github.
- The fourth thing that I found confusing was the "Start micro break" feature from the Focus Bear app on Mac. Specifically, when I first downloaded the app and was walked through the setup guidance of the app, there were no guidance for that feature and the feature itself was not being mentioned in that process. Then when I finished the guidance process, I saw that feature appearing right below the "Start Focus Session" option. Additionally, when I clicked on that feature, it shows "Next break in 21 mintutes" which also made me confused because I didn't know why did that number appear and when did I set it up. I expected to have a guidance for that feature and potetntially, there should be a small question mark on that "Start Micro Break" page which could briefly explain the meaning of that number.
- The fifth thing that I think Focus Bear could improve is the "Help" section in the Mac version by allowing users to click on the whole tutorial blocks in the "Tutorials" section instead of just designing a small clickable button on the top-left corner. Currently, each article in that section can only be clicked on if users click on a small button on the top left corner of each article. Personally, it is quite inconvenient and could cause mis-click which could make users feel frustrated.
