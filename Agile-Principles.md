# Research & learn:
- What is Agile & how it differs from traditional project management:
1. Agile: iterative, incremental delivery with continuous feedback and adaptation
2. Traditional (waterfall): sequential phases (requirements → design → build → test → release) planned upfront, with little room to adapt mid-project
3. Agile embraces changing requirements even late in development; waterfall treats change as costly/disruptive
- Core values: Individuals and interactions over processes and tools, working software over comprehensive documentation, customer collaboration over contract negotiation, responding to change over following a plan
- Scrum vs. Kanban
1. Scrum: fixed-length sprints, defined roles (Scrum Master, PM, dev team), sprint planning/review/retro ceremonies, team commits to a batch of work per sprint
2. Kanban: continuous flow, no fixed sprints, work pulled from a prioritized backlog as capacity allows, flexible re-prioritization at any time
3. Scrum suits teams needing predictable release cadences; Kanban suits teams with frequently shifting priorities or smaller/faster-moving teams
- Why Focus Bear likely leans Kanban:
1. Small team, needs flexibility to reprioritize quickly
2. Continuous shipping/bug-fixing doesn't fit neatly into fixed sprints
3. Less ceremony overhead suits a lean startup pace

# Reflection:
1. Biggest benefits: faster feedback loops, adaptability, visible progress, less wasted work on outdated requirements
2. Biggest challenges: can lead to scope creep without discipline, requires strong communication to avoid chaos, harder to give firm long-term deadlines
3. Agile principles helping your role: continuous small increments make it easier to get feedback early rather than building the wrong thing for weeks
